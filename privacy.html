Privacy and data handling

Updated October 24, 2026. Katoptron is a public beta.

Routes and external services

When you enter addresses, your browser sends location searches to the selected third-party routing and geocoding provider (currently Geoapify), which receives location queries and route coordinates. Those third parties can receive your IP address, coordinates, route details and ordinary request metadata under their own privacy practices. Using latitude,longitude coordinates skips geocoding but not routing.

Your browser contacts Supabase to query published camera evidence in geographical regions along the route. Katoptron's application does not write your plate number, selected route or synthetic timeline into its database. Hosting, third-party maps and service providers may maintain request logs. Do not submit sensitive routes if this is a concern.

Simulation

Katoptron never accesses real Flock license-plate reads, vehicle movement histories or private Flock data. A simulated encounter is not proof a camera is online, can see a particular road segment, or read anyone's plate.

Accounts and submissions

The public map does not require sign-in and Katoptron's own map script does not intentionally store route searches in cookies or browser storage. The separate editorial dashboard may use authentication sessions. A signed-in community-submission form accepts up to three reports per account per 24 hours. These reports contain contributor account IDs, submitted coordinates, descriptions, dates and optional source URLs. They are stored privately for research and are not automatically published. The optional, separately unchecked publication-consent control permits independently authenticated evidence source URLs and camera coordinates to appear in a public case only after approval; it never permits disclosure of the contributor's account identity. Without that consent the derived report case stays private, even if its evidence later qualifies. Reports can be withdrawn through the contributor's report history. Withdrawing a report revokes evidence derived from it and blocks new reviewer access to leftover photo objects. The form attempts to erase attached photographs; if deletion fails, the contributor can use Retry photo deletion on the withdrawn report. Private links issued before withdrawal may remain usable until their short expiration. Deleting a Supabase account removes account-linked report rows, but deletion of separately stored images is not guaranteed by account deletion alone. Original private review audit entries may be retained for integrity and accountability. Existing crowdsourced sightings also stay in a private research queue.

Submitted photographs

Optional JPEG, PNG, or WebP evidence images are limited to 3 MB each and stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. They are accessible to their contributor and authorized reviewers. Reviewers may generate short-lived private access links. Do not upload photographs showing identifiable individuals, number plates, homes, or other private information. Storage providers may record ordinary request metadata.

Public government program directory

A separate public list shows agency-level Flock evidence without coordinates; it does not locate or track individuals or cameras. The dated citations and historical program termination indicators are original public-government information.

Coverage limitations

Source data can be incomplete, stale or inaccurate. No pin on the map does not mean surveillance is absent. This site should not be used to determine a vehicle's safety or anonymity.
