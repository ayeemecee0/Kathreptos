Camera evidence methodology

Updated October 22, 2026.

Source-backed exact points

A pin requires authority-bound original government GIS evidence that explicitly names a Flock device or Flock camera layer. An exact pin represents coordinates published in that source, not an independent physical inspection or proof that the device is operational. Popups link to source records and include when the source was last checked.

Approximate areas

A programme without source-verified individual camera pins may still qualify for a mapped area if its original government disclosure directly links Flock-specific, independently georeferenced GIS polygon boundaries. Approximate areas show only those original polygons, never invented circles around a city, assumed street corridors, or inactive/terminated deployments.

An area needs credible primary-source evidence of deployment without disclosed individual positions. Approximate coverage is drawn as an uncertainty region, never as a fabricated camera pin. No such regions have qualified yet.

Documented jurisdictions with unknown camera locations

Katoptron separately displays dated original-government Flock program evidence when exact camera coordinates have not been verified. These descriptions do not create camera pins, imply camera sightlines, or prove current operation. Terminated programs are explicitly labeled historical.

Evidence history

The public map provides original publication and subsequent official-source recheck dates for currently verified GIS camera records. Rechecking a source does not prove a device remains operational. History for records whose authority was withdrawn is not displayed as current evidence.

Official government and endorsed external GIS evidence

Each camera point must have original georeferenced evidence. A first-party GIS source can qualify when an authenticated original government Flock disclosure directly hyperlinks its exact .gov-operated layer; third-party Esri hosting instead requires a separately verified original government endorsement and original ArcGIS organization metadata. Title matches or map links alone are not proof. Automatic source rechecks retain original evidence history and withdraw source-specific pins when a successfully rechecked official disclosure removes the formerly endorsed Flock camera layer. Network outages do not by themselves revoke records.

Independent evidence tiers

Katoptron now supports a separate reviewed claim and evidence-attestation ledger. Source documents may establish a government point through an original government GIS feed, independently authenticated government ArcGIS organization or original agency document with georeferenced coordinates. These routes all require independent source verification, bounded coordinates and an authorized review. Existing original-government points remain in the canonical verified camera inventory.

A different public tier, independently corroborated, requires at least two reviewed sources from different underlying origin families, with coordinates agreeing within roughly 50 meters of the claimed point. At least one must be direct field evidence, original government material or independent firsthand reporting rather than recycled map aggregations alone. Missing, stale, withdrawn, or rejected corroboration unpublishes a claim automatically. All public corroborated points appear on an optional map layer, OFF by default, and never contribute to government-verified totals or default route simulated encounters. Underlying review evidence remains private where unverified; a case's public summary and source citation do not prove camera operation.

Private unverified reports

Community photos, OSM nodes, third-party ArcGIS sources and news about Flock purchases remain unpublished as camera positions until independently corroborated. Matching OSM to a government pin adds supporting evidence, not an extra pin. Credits: OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License.

Route simulations

Route proximity is measured to the actual modeled road segments. The 120-meter threshold is illustrative and is NOT a Flock manufacturer's reading range or a statement about direction of view. Time estimates are derived from the route's estimated total travel duration and distance fraction. No actual plate detections are available. Fictional samples require explicit opt-in.

Unknown coverage

Missing or unsuccessful evidence searches produce an unknown result, not proof a road lacks cameras. Camera inventory remains nationwide in scope but highly incomplete in collection. Published GIS records are rechecked and withdrawn when original authority is revoked.
